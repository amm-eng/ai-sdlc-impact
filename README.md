# AI SDLC Impact Framework

A practical guide for assessing whether AI-enabled software delivery is creating meaningful value while preserving quality, reliability, security, developer experience, and sound economics.

It is intended for team-, service-, and initiative-level decisions. It helps frame an AI intervention, select comparable measures, interpret outcomes alongside guardrails, and decide whether to scale, adjust, or stop a use case.

## Documents

- [Framework overview](ai-sdlc-impact-framework-overview.md) — the evaluation model, minimum metric set, decision rules, and reporting template.
- [Metric reference](ai-sdlc-metric-reference.md) — definitions, units, calculation guidance, and collection approaches for the measures used by the framework.

## What this is

This is an opinionated guideline for moving AI impact assessment forward. It brings together learnings from published research sources with the author's own observations from applying and refining the approach in production delivery settings.

It offers one possible path: evaluate meaningful delivery or value outcomes, use exposure and flow data to understand mechanisms, and treat reliability, security, governance, developer experience, and economics as guardrails rather than trade-offs against speed.

## What this is not

This is not a claim to a new framework, a definitive research result, or an extensively validated methodology. It should not be treated as a universal benchmark, an individual-developer productivity score, or evidence that AI created an outcome without a credible comparison.

Adapt the measures, definitions, thresholds, and comparison approach to the delivery context. Read the cited research sources directly and make the assumptions and limitations of each assessment explicit.
